PHASE 5: WAKE WORD VERSUS NORMAL-CONVERSATION DISAMBIGUATION

Design separate states for passive wake detection and active conversational interpretation.

Required lifecycle:

- WAKE_WORD_DISABLED;
- WAKE_WORD_CONFIGURED;
- WAKE_WORD_READY;
- WAKE_WORD_LISTENING;
- WAKE_WORD_CANDIDATE;
- WAKE_WORD_DETECTED;
- COMMAND_LISTENING;
- REQUEST_FINALIZING;
- PROCESSING;
- SPEAKING;
- FOLLOW_UP_LISTENING;
- SUSPENDED;
- PRIVACY_BLOCKED;
- UNAVAILABLE.

Passive mode rules:

- Only a canonical or approved bounded wake candidate may activate.
- The wake phrase itself is not executed as a portal command.
- Text after the wake phrase in the same utterance becomes the request.
- A bare wake word opens one bounded command-listening turn.
- No connector/tool/model request begins before activation is adjudicated.
- Low-confidence or ambiguous candidates do not activate.
- Repeated low-confidence candidates do not automatically weaken thresholds.

Active-session rules:

- During a valid foreground conversational session or bounded follow-up window, natural references to ONYX/NOVA do not require a new wake word.
- Direct vocative address at utterance start may switch the response character only when clearly addressed and allowed by policy.
- Mentioning ONYX/NOVA in discussion must not switch character or activate commands.

Examples that should activate when passive and confidence/policy gates pass:

- “Hey Onyx, open Mail.”
- “Onyx.” followed by a command inside the bounded listening window.
- “Noba, show my tasks.” when `noba` is an approved NOVA pronunciation drift and not ambiguous.
- “Hey Nova, continue.”

Examples that should not automatically activate or switch character:

- “The ONYX architecture uses provider-neutral adapters.”
- “I was discussing NOVA yesterday.”
- “Compare ONYX and NOVA voice settings.”
- Audio containing the word in background media without sufficient confidence/foreground evidence.
- Similar ordinary words from the negative/confusable corpus.

Examples requiring clarification or no activation:

- A candidate acoustically compatible with both characters.
- A drift alias with low confidence.
- An utterance containing only an ambiguous similar word.
- A command directed at no character while wake-word-only mode is configured.

The architecture must separate:

WAKE_DETECTION
≠ CHARACTER_SELECTION
≠ INTENT_RESOLUTION
≠ AUTHENTICATION
≠ AUTHORIZATION
≠ APPROVAL
≠ EXECUTION
