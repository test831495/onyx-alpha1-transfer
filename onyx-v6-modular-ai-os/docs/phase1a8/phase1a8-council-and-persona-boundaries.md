# Phase 1A.8 Wave 3D: Council and Persona Boundaries

Council Mode remains contract-only and deterministic-test-only. It is not a third merged persona, does not grant memory or connector access, does not change ONYX or NOVA P0 baselines, and does not replace Rahul approval authority.

ONYX and NOVA remain separate personas with immutable P0 baselines. `ONYX_NOVA_COUNCIL` is a coordination presence mode only, and the Council contract enforces distinct ONYX and NOVA participant identities and separate persona metadata references. Shared governed task facts are stable task context only; they are not private persona memory and they cannot become P0. Council agreement and disagreement are both preserved in the recommendation package, and the recommendation package cannot silently discard disagreement.

Character contributions preserve attribution to the originating persona and remain advisory inputs, not approval. Council convergence validates one workflow ID, one runtime ID, one approval lineage, one checkpoint lineage, one evidence lineage, and one promotion path while preserving P0 immutability and the original permission and connector scope boundaries. Rahul approval remains required for any Council recommendation that reaches approval state.
